## Target Sectors

### 1. Life Science Reagents &amp; Specialized Consumables
* Specialized biochemicals, antibodies, enzymes, extraction kits, cell culture media, chromatography columns, and laboratory plastics.
* Embedded in validated customer standard operating procedures (SOPs) with high switching costs.
* High gross margin profile with proprietary formulations or manufacturing protocols.

### 2. Laboratory Instrumentation &amp; Automation
* Benchtop analytical instruments, sample preparation systems, automated liquid handlers, optical/spectroscopy detectors, and QC hardware.
* Razor / razorblade model with strong pull-through of proprietary consumables and multi-year service contracts.
* Mission-critical reliability within core research and testing workflows.

### 3. Specialty Analytical &amp; Contract Research Services (CRO)
* Bioanalytical testing, custom assay development, protein characterization, stability testing, and GLP-compliant contract laboratory services.
* Sticky, multi-year relationships with biopharma sponsors and research institutions.
* Opportunities to expand capacity through automated high-throughput workflows.

## A Different Kind of Buyer: The Operator Alternative

Traditional private equity firms often treat niche life science companies as financial assets—slashing technical overhead, moving production, or rolling them up into an impersonal conglomerate.

| Traditional Private Equity | Dan Catlin (Operator-Led) |
| :--- | :--- |
