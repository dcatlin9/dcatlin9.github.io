## The Translation Bridge: From Discovery Bench to P&amp;L

In life sciences, the most costly friction occurs at the intersection of breakthrough scientific discovery and commercial scaling. Commercial executives frequently lack the domain literacy to pressure-test laboratory claims, while brilliant bench scientists often struggle to translate assay mechanisms into scalable commercial models, pricing matrices, and sales channels.

**I operate directly in that chasm.**

Having spent a decade solving protein structures, designing enzyme kinetics assays, and scaling technical workflows, I understand how scientists think, what core lab directors require, and why certain technologies fail to gain adoption despite impressive bench data. 

Through an Executive MBA focused on corporate valuation, leveraged buyouts, capital structure, and market entry, I pair that scientific depth with executive commercial discipline. Whether evaluating a target for acquisition, stress-testing an assay's reproducibility for investors, or restructuring a commercial product line, the fundamental habit is the same:

> *"The discipline of asking the right question travels further than the subject matter it started in."*
