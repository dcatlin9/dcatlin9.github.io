# Biotech Commercialization &amp; Technical Due Diligence

> De-risking life science investments and accelerating product adoption. I advise private equity sponsors, search fund searchers, venture capital firms, and high-growth biotech executive teams on evaluating technical validity, workflow friction, and commercial defensibility.

---

## Core Advisory Capabilities

### 1. Technical Due Diligence for M&amp;A and Growth Equity
*Tailored for Private Equity Sponsors, Search Funds, Family Offices, and Acquirers*

Independent, rigorous audits of scientific claims, proprietary assays, and instrumentation before term sheets or closing. Evaluating whether an asset's lab performance translates to commercial reality.

* **Assay Robustness &amp; Reproducibility:** Scrutinizing analytical validation data, coefficient of variation (CV), limits of detection (LoD), and batch-to-batch lot consistency.
* **Lab Workflow &amp; Operational Friction:** Assessing hands-on technician time, sample preparation complexity, specialized equipment dependencies, and automation bottlenecks.
* **IP &amp; Moat Defensibility:** Determining whether proprietary chemistry, enzyme engineering, or specialized hardware prevents fast-follower commoditization.
* **Supply Chain &amp; Scalability:** Auditing raw material single-sourcing (custom enzymes, monoclonal antibodies, microfluidics) and yield stability under scale.

---

### 2. Life Science Commercialization &amp; GTM Strategy
*Tailored for Biotech Founders, Tool Developers, and Growth Companies*

Guiding scientific instruments, consumables, and reagents from prototype validation into repeatable, high-margin revenue engines.

* **Product-Market Fit &amp; Persona Alignment:** Aligning value propositions between Academic PIs (grant-funded, publication-driven), Core Lab Directors (throughput, reliability, unit economics), and Pharma R&amp;D (regulatory compliance, data integrity, vendor stability).
* **Pricing Architecture &amp; Unit Economics:** Structuring razor/razorblade pricing models, reagent consumable attachment rates, and annual preventative maintenance service contracts.
* **Distribution vs. Direct Sales:** Evaluating when to leverage established global scientific distributors versus building a specialized direct technical sales force.
* **Key Opinion Leader (KOL) Beta Sites:** Structuring early-access beta evaluation programs, co-authored validation papers, and customer advocacy networks.

---

### 3. Translational Strategy &amp; Pipeline Optimization
*Tailored for Executive Leadership, Boards, and R&amp;D Steering Committees*

Preventing wasteful R&amp;D cycles by aligning laboratory project roadmaps directly with market demand, customer workflow friction, and buyer willingness-to-pay.

* **Stage-Gate Portfolio Prioritization:** Establishing objective technical and commercial milestones for advancing, pivoting, or deprioritizing internal R&amp;D programs.
* **Voice-of-Customer (VoC) Discovery:** Conducting structured, peer-to-peer technical interviews with bench scientists and purchasing committees to confirm market need.
* **R&amp;D-to-Commercial Vocabulary Alignment:** Bridging the communication gap between research scientists, product managers, and commercial sales teams.
* **Interim Commercial Direction:** Providing fractional leadership to execute commercial launches during critical inflection windows.
